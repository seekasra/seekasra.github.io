Reading

Why the midpoint? Dividing by the average of the two values rather than the starting value gives the same elasticity whichever direction the price moves, so A→B and B→A agree. The sign is negative because price and quantity move in opposite directions along a demand curve; it is the size, |PED|, that tells you elastic (>1), unit (=1) or inelastic (<1).
