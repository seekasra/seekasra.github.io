Where we are

Why SRAS slopes up but LRAS is vertical. With spare capacity firms can raise output without much price pressure; near full employment bottlenecks push prices up sharply. In the long run only the economy’s productive capacity (labour, capital, resources, technology) sets output, so LRAS is vertical at the natural rate, and it shifts right as the economy grows.
