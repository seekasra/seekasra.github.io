Closing a recessionary or inflationary gap

Potential output is where LRAS stands. When aggregate demand leaves the economy below it there is a recessionary gap; above it, an inflationary gap. Demand-side policy moves AD to close the gap; supply-side policy moves LRAS itself. Questions 4 and 7.

Twin objectives. Governments aim for short-term stability, damping the swings of real GDP around LRAS, and long-term growth, moving LRAS itself to the right. Monetary and fiscal policy work on the first; supply-side policy on the second.
