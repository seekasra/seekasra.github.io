What the structures share and where they differ. All three firms choose output where MR = MC. Free entry (perfect and monopolistic competition) drives profit to zero in the long run; barriers (monopoly) do not. A horizontal demand curve means P = MC and no scope to price above cost; a downward-sloping one means P > MC, excess capacity and a reason to advertise.
