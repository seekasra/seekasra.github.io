Elasticity (slope) controls

Flatter = more price-elastic; steeper = more inelastic. Curves pivot through the original equilibrium, so slopes change how far price and quantity move, not the direction.

Rule of thumb. With a downward-sloping demand curve and an upward-sloping supply curve, a shift in demand moves price and quantity in the same direction, while a shift in supply moves them in opposite directions. Try shifting both at once: one of the two outcomes becomes ambiguous and depends on which shift is larger.
