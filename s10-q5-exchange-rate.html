Q6 in the same logic. If the euro appreciates, a US pension fund earning euros is happy (more dollars per euro); German exporters are not (dearer abroad), though German firms importing inputs from outside the eurozone benefit; Australian tourists get fewer euros for their dollars; a British firm buying property in Europe pays more in pounds, but would not care if the property were elsewhere.
