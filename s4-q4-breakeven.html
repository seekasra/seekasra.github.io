Breakeven point and margin of safety

Each jacket contributes its selling price minus its variable cost (production cost plus sales commission) towards fixed costs. Breakeven is where total contribution just covers fixed costs. Two ways to read the margin of safety. In units it is expected sales minus breakeven sales; in money it is the same gap valued at the selling price. Both give the same percentage of expected sales. The bigger the margin, the further sales can fall before the firm makes a loss.
